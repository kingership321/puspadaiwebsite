# Development Seed Data

Create realistic fictional data for development.

Do not use real people's personal data.

Include:

## Cities

At least 5 cities.

## Areas

At least 5 neighborhoods per city.

## Agencies

At least 10 agencies.

## Agents

At least 20 agents.

## Properties

At least 100 properties distributed across:

- Rent
- Sale
- Apartments
- Houses
- Condos
- Townhouses

Use varied:

- Prices
- Bedrooms
- Bathrooms
- Areas
- Images
- Amenities
- Locations
- Listing dates

## Seed requirements

Every property should have:

- Valid slug
- At least 3 images
- Location
- Price
- Property type
- Basic facts
- Description
- Provider

Seed data must make all important UI states demonstrable.
